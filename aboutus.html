About Aoun Library

We are a neighborhood library with a simple goal: help everyone find a good book. This site shows what is on the shelf right now, so you can check before you make the trip.

What you can do here

  • Filter the catalog by category and by whether a book is available.
  • See at a glance which books are available, checked out, or on hold.
  • Request a book we don't have yet.

Everyone is welcome

This site works with keyboards, screen readers, and phones. If something is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it.
